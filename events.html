Local Bitcoiners

A Bitcoin podcast about meetup culture. Hosts Reed and Rev Hodl sit down with organizers from across the country on how they find, build, and grow local Bitcoin communities — not price charts, real-world grassroots adoption.

Run a meetup and want to come on, or curious about Grassroots Bitcoin and the quarterly organizer calls? localbitcoiners@gmail.com
